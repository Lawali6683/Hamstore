1. Enter the code from the office1. Shigar da lambar da ofis ya ba ka

The office sent you a code that starts with DR. It opens the delivery address and the route.Ofis ya tura maka lamba da ke farawa da DR. Tana buɗe adireshin isarwa da hanya.

2. Customer's slip code2. Lambar slip ɗin kwastoma

When you reach the customer, scan the QR code on his slip or type the code on it. This opens the goods he paid for.Idan ka isa wurin kwastoma, ka duba QR code ɗin da ke slip ɗinsa ko ka rubuta lambar. Wannan yana buɗe kayan da ya biya.
